# Seasons

Author Spaces compete in independent weekly and seasonal Gold pools. Weekly rewards use per-quest completion diversity and Karma bands; seasonal rewards use same-quarter final consensus ratings with a 12-quest qualification threshold and linear Rank Shares for the rewarded top 10%.

At UTC settlement, allocated rewards become immutable pending receipts for the
Author Space. Only its owner can withdraw them to a personal balance through the
existing Space flow. They are not converted into treasury Silver. Weekly and
seasonal pools are funded by the [Unified Reward Budget](../assets/reward-budget.md).
See [Quest Creation Rewards](../quest-mining/creation/rewards.md) and
[Seasons](../quest-mining/seasons.md) for ranking and withdrawal rules.
