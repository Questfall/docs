# Seasons

Author Spaces compete in independent weekly and seasonal Gold pools. Weekly rewards use per-quest completion diversity and Karma bands; seasonal rewards use same-quarter final consensus ratings with a 12-quest qualification threshold and linear Rank Shares for the rewarded top 10%.

At UTC settlement, every allocated Author Gold reward is converted at the fixed rate of `1 Gold = 10 Silver` and credited to the Author Space treasury with an immutable receipt. See [Quest Creation Rewards](../quest-mining/creation/rewards.md) and [Seasons](../quest-mining/seasons.md) for the complete formulas.
