# Seasonal Rewards

The current seasonal rules are documented in [Seasons](../quest-mining/seasons.md).
Seasons are UTC calendar quarters. Miner and author pools accumulate separately
from the [Unified Reward Budget](../assets/reward-budget.md), with target QFT
weights of 11.2 and 2.8 respectively. The earlier 10% three-category design has
been superseded.
