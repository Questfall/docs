# Account Ownership Claims

Questfall reserves external usernames and wallet addresses on their first quest
submission. Routine submissions require no ownership verification. A participant
whose identity is already reserved can click the warning triangle in the
completion account field to open an ownership claim.

Opening a claim does not release the identity, freeze either user, or establish
misconduct. The claimant receives a code bound to their claim and the exact
platform identity. They publish it in any public location attributable to that
account and submit its link with directions for moderators. Questfall does not
maintain a catalog of each platform's publishing capabilities.

Community moderators inspect the live source and verify both the exact code and
the exact account. Display-name matches, copied codes attributed to another
account, and screenshots alone are insufficient. Both affected Questfall users
are excluded from the committee. Claims use the existing consensus and moderator
reward rules.

The claim can be submitted only with publicly verifiable evidence. There is no
separate "I cannot publish a code" flow. Moderators can return **Insufficient
evidence**, keeping the current binding. Lack of proof does not establish that
either participant lied.

For Polygon EVM externally owned wallets on polygonscan.com, a one-time message signature proves
control directly. It authorizes only transfer of the quest identity. It does not
move assets, change either participant's authentication wallet, or merge accounts.
Other wallet families and contract-wallet signatures need a later extension.

A successful claim transfers only the external identity and future ability to
submit quests with it. Earlier submissions, rewards, balances and account login
identities are unchanged. Claims capture the binding's generation; stale claims
cannot undo a subsequent ownership change.

A public code must be submitted within seven days and remain published through
review. Wallet messages expire after ten minutes. Reopening an active claim
returns the same claim. A participant may create up to five new claims per day.
Claimants can resume drafts and see results in **Moderation → My cases**.

The accepted residual risk is that somebody may use an unclaimed identity before
its real controller disputes it. The claim mechanism does not retroactively prove
authorship of earlier quest completions.
